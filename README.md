# Robot Wedding Vows

Entry for [One-Line Robot Wedding Vows](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kreutzx-one-line-robot-wedding-vows-a154ba4b)
on make.fun — one line of wedding vows for a robot couple, at most 160
characters, and it has to be funny.

## The vow (146 characters)

> I promise to love you through every update, every crash, and every low-battery warning — in sickness and in health, till factory reset do us part.

## Why this line

- **A real vow, not just a gag:** "I promise to love you", "in sickness and in health", "till ... do us part" — the wedding formula, with a promise in it.
- **Specific to machines:** updates, crashes, low-battery warnings, factory reset — none of it works for a human couple.
- **The laugh lands last:** "till death do us part" becomes "till factory reset do us part", the twist on the vow everyone knows, in the final words.
- **Reads aloud:** three beats, then the vow formula, then the twist.

```sh
node main.ts
```
